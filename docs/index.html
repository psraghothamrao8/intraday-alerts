💡 How to execute an alert (Quick Guide)
1. Entry Order: In your broker app (Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, etc.), place a Limit Order at or below the max entry price before the validity time expires.
2. Safety Stop (SL-M): Immediately place an SL-M (Stop-Loss Market) order at the Safety SL price. This is your hard safety net against unexpected sudden drops.
3. Monitor via Bot: You don't need to stare at charts. The bot watches the 5-min candles ("Thesis Stop"). If the thesis breaks or the profit target is hit, you will receive a 🔴 SELL alert.
4. Exit & Clean up: When you receive the exit alert (or at the intraday cutoff like 15:07), exit the trade in your broker and cancel your pending Safety SL order.
